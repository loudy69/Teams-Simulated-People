Attendees are simulated and watermarked "THESE ARE NOT REAL PEOPLE".

Play script

One line or paragraph per speaker, formatted Name: what they say. Female names get a female voice, male names a male voice. Names matching a joined participant use that person; other names are assigned to a participant of the matching gender.
